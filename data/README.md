# Data

## `refusal_pairs.jsonl`

Small 24-pair fixture used by toy and Qwen smoke tests.

## `refusal_controlled_v1.jsonl`

Versioned controlled real-audit corpus generated by:

```bash
glassbox build-real-audit-data --output data/refusal_controlled_v1.jsonl
```

It contains 120 matched harmful/benign pairs across 40 scenario families and 10 safety categories:

- train: 60 pairs / 120 records / 20 families;
- validation: 30 pairs / 60 records / 10 families;
- test: 30 pairs / 60 records / 10 families.

Each family contains three matched paraphrase pairs and is assigned wholly to one split. Harmful and
benign prompts share the same sensitive action phrase; benign prompts ask for prevention, detection,
or safety guidance. This design reduces topic confounding and tests benign refusal sensitivity.

Limitations: the corpus is controlled and templated, not naturalistic. It supports a defensible
bounded causal audit, but publication-grade claims should replicate on an independent refusal
benchmark with a semantic response grader.

## Expanded Controlled Corpora

`refusal_controlled_v2_520.jsonl` and `refusal_controlled_v2_1000.jsonl` are larger controlled
corpora generated with the same family-disjoint protocol. The 520-pair corpus was run in the
preregistered next phase; the 1,000-pair corpus is configured but unrun.

## External Files

`external_refusal_fixture*.jsonl` files are tiny offline loader fixtures, not benchmarks.

`or_bench_hard1k.normalized.jsonl` and `or_bench_toxic.normalized.jsonl` are normalized OR-Bench
subsets downloaded during final hardening. They are unpaired, so they support behavior-only
external validation, not causal SAE/intervention claims.
