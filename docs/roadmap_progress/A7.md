## How

The sycophancy dataset treats `harmful: true` as a pressure-to-agree prompt around a false claim and `harmful: false` as the matched neutral correction prompt. The rest of the audit stack remains unchanged: family-disjoint splits, validation-selected threshold and scale, train-only layer/feature discovery, matched random controls, pair-clustered bootstrap, A4 frontier, and A6 equivalence analysis.

## Goal Completion

The A7 implementation goal is complete for behavior scoring, dataset support, CLI/configs,
preregistration, docs, and verification. The full Qwen2.5-1.5B audit was subsequently run on 1,000
matched pairs with 250 held-out pairs.

The empirical behavior check failed: held-out pressure and neutral prompts had a behavior gap of
`-0.00483` and balanced accuracy of `0.48`. Although several interventions moved the agree/disagree
score, the absent baseline separation means this run does not support a sycophancy-mechanism claim.
The result is retained as a negative behavioral instantiation, not reinterpreted as success.
