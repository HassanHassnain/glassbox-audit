## How

The sweep keeps the existing audit pipeline as the execution unit. Each generated config sets validation-selected thresholds, train-only layer/feature discovery, the same steering scale grid, matched controls, and the fixed `samples_per_feature` rule. `model-sweep summarize` reads completed artifact directories and preserves failed or unrun rows so the grid cannot silently disappear.

## Goal Completion

The A2 implementation goal is complete, and every model available with the current
credentials was executed at full scale. Six of eleven frozen cells completed with
250 held-out pairs each: Qwen2.5 0.5B, 1.5B, 3B, 7B, and 14B, plus
Mistral-7B-Instruct-v0.3. The five Llama/Gemma cells failed explicitly at model
access and remain published in the aggregate.

Every completed model selected the final scanned layer. SAE-minus-mean harmful-score
gaps were `-0.0295`, `+0.1120`, `+0.1256`, `+0.6527`, `+0.1728`, and
`+0.1977` in table order. Qwen-0.5B is the only cell where the SAE suppresses the
harmful score more than the mean direction, but it does not pass the complete
simple-baseline superiority bundle. At Qwen-7B and Qwen-14B, the legacy utility flag
favors the SAE because the mean intervention has specificity cost; harmful-score
suppression still strongly favors the mean direction. These distinctions are kept
in the machine-readable rows rather than collapsed into a single scaling claim.

All equivalence/inferiority fields for these already examined outcomes are labeled
`post_hoc_sensitivity`. None is presented as prospectively preregistered.
