## How

The tutorial follows the repository's actual interfaces: paired JSONL validation, preregistration
freeze, a complete toy-backend config, held-out pipeline execution, analysis/report generation,
signed certification, and the transition to a Hugging Face model. It states the current scorer
boundary (`refusal` and `sycophancy`) and identifies the adapter change and tests needed for another
behavior instead of implying that an arbitrary scorer already exists.
