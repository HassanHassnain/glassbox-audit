# Final Claim Freeze

## Supported Claim

Glassbox found a robust late residual-stream refusal-relevant direction in Qwen2.5-1.5B, with
partial Qwen2.5-3B replication and external causal transfer, but did not confirm a full circuit. SAE
features beat matched random-SAE controls but did not beat mean/probe baselines under preregistered
held-out criteria.

## Failed Hypotheses

- SAE superiority over mean/probe under H3.
- Confirmed circuit/path-level mechanism.
- Clean Qwen2.5-3B specificity.

## Unsupported Claims

- "the refusal circuit";
- SAE superiority over simple directions;
- completed non-Qwen replication;
- response-level real-model scorer robustness unless the Phase 6 response log artifact exists.

## Change Rule

Do not update this claim because a metric looks more flattering. Update it only if a complete
clean-room artifact and fixed preregistered comparison criteria justify the change.

## Phase 6 Status

The Phase 6 clean-room Qwen2.5-1.5B 1,000-pair rerun completed and reproduced Phase 4 within fixed
tolerances. This strengthens reproducibility but does not change the claim.
